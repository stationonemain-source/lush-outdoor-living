The studio

A pool is the most permanent thing in a garden. We design the garden around it, then build both.

Lush Outdoor Living is a small Houston studio for pools, landscapes and outdoor living. Every project is designed by a registered landscape architect, which means the grading, drainage, planting and the water are drawn as one plan, not stitched together by separate trades.

The same studio builds what it draws, so the design and the build answer to one person, from the first site visit to the first evening by the water.

    Why a landscape architect

    The water is only half the yard

    A pool contractor builds a pool. A landscape architect designs the whole outdoor space it sits in: how the ground drains after a Houston storm, which trees stay, where the shade falls at four in the afternoon, how the terrace meets the house. Lush does both, which is why the finished yards read as gardens with water in them, not pools with grass around them.
